Refund Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Refunds are handled manually by our team. Except for the automatic cases listed below, you need to ask us by direct message on Instagram. We answer as soon as we can.

Gem packs

  • A gem pack you have not used at all can be refunded in full within 7 days of the purchase.
  • Gems that have been spent on items, lots or house styles cannot be refunded, and a pack from which any gems were spent is not refundable.

Businesses

  • If all spots were already taken when your payment arrived, you are refunded in full automatically.
  • A business stays on the map for good. It is never closed because of low results, so business purchases are final and are not refunded (except for the cases on this page: spots already taken, double charges or something not delivered).

Mistakes and double charges

If you were charged twice or something was not delivered, tell us and we will fix it or refund you in full.

How to ask

Send a direct message to @fillthemap.usa on Instagram with the email you used at checkout and what you bought. Please write to us before opening a dispute with your bank, so we can solve it faster.

How you receive it

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days. When a refund is made, the gems or business it paid for are removed from your account.
