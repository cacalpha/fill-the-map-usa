Privacy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This map shows Instagram usernames of people who publicly commented on our posts with their city. If you buy something in the game we also keep the small amount of data described below. We do not sell your data and we do not run ads or tracking cookies.

What we show on the map

Your public @, the city you wrote, your coins and your office. It comes from public comments on our Instagram posts.

What we keep when you buy

  • The email you give at checkout, to create your account and contact you about your purchase.
  • What you bought, when, and for how much, plus your gems, items, house layout and businesses.
  • A hash of your recovery code (never the code itself).
  • For security, your IP address is kept briefly to limit repeated sign-in attempts.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card number and bank details go to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to that data.

Cookies

When you sign in we set one cookie that keeps you signed in. It is needed for the shop to work. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Where it is stored

The game is hosted on Vercel and its database runs on Supabase. Both act as service providers for us.

Your choices

  • Remove your house or your account: send us a direct message on Instagram and we will remove it. Items already paid for are covered by the Refund Policy.
  • See or correct your data: message us and we will help.

Age

Purchases are for people 18 or older, or with a parent or guardian's permission. The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Direct message @fillthemap.usa on Instagram.
